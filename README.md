# 💸 SpendMate  

**SpendMate** is a modern **mobile application** designed to simplify expense and task management for **roommates, friends, and groups**. It makes **bill splitting, task assignment, and spending tracking effortless**, while providing **analytics and insights** to help users manage shared expenses wisely.  

With features like **expense categorization**, **bill reminders**, and **conditional task segregation** (based on jobs, internships, dietary restrictions, etc.), SpendMate offers a comprehensive solution for anyone sharing frequent costs.  

---

## ✨ Features  

- 🧾 **Bill & Task Splitting** – Share expenses and assign tasks fairly  
- 📊 **Spending Insights & Analytics** – Get detailed breakdowns of your spending habits  
- 🗂️ **Categorization of Expenses** – Organize costs into categories for better tracking  
- 👥 **Group & Individual Balances** – View who owes whom in groups or individually  
- ⏰ **Bill Reminders & Notifications** – Never miss a due payment  
- 🔔 **Push Notifications & Alerts** – Real-time updates on balances, reminders, and tasks  
- ⚖️ **Conditional Task Segregation** – Assign chores and contributions based on conditions like:  
  - On-campus jobs 💼  
  - Internships 👩‍💻  
  - Dietary restrictions 🍽️  
  - Custom rules set by group members  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  

- **Frontend:** Flutter (cross-platform mobile app)  
- **Backend & Database:** Firebase (Realtime Database / Firestore)  
- **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication (Email/Google Sign-In)  
- **Notifications:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications  
- **Analytics:** Custom dashboards using Flutter charts  

---

## 🎯 Why SpendMate?  

- Solves the **common problem of shared expenses and chores** among roommates and friends  
- Provides **transparency & accountability** in group spending  
- Goes beyond expense-sharing by providing **insights into spending habits**  
- Built with **scalability and cross-platform mobile experience** in mind
